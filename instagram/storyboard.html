What the reels will look like

Each row is one reel: the first frame (the hook), one middle frame, and the last frame. Every reel shows one piece of your work: an ad creative, a product photo, a product page, or how a creative strategist thinks. Products are generic (coffee, candles, pillows, protein, skincare, pet food) so any brand owner sees themselves in it.

Look: Plus Jakarta Sans, ink background, green for the fix, amber for the problem. Voice: a calm AI voice-over now, your own voice later. Numbers marked "pulled on posting day" come from the Meta Ad Library or search data on the day we post, never made up.
