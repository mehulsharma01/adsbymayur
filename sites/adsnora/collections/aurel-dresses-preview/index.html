Finding your dress

Not sure on length? Our minis sit 2 cm above mid-thigh on a 172 cm model, midis land at mid-calf, and maxis brush the ankle. Each product page lists the exact centre-back length in centimetres.

How do AUREL dresses fit?

True to size with a close fit through the bodice. Denim styles have 2% stretch; if you are between sizes in rigid denim, size up.

Can I return a sale dress?

Yes. Sale items follow the same 30-day free return window as full-price pieces.

When will my order arrive?

Express orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day and arrive in 1 to 3 business days.
